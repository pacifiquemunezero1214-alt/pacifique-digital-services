Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Pacifique Digital Services collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website and digital services.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Pacifique Digital Services is committed to respecting the privacy of its customers and website visitors. This policy explains how personal information is handled when you create an account, request a service, communicate with us or use our platform.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will request your consent before processing information that requires consent under applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Service information: Details and instructions you provide when requesting a service.
  • Payment information: Payment method, amount, transaction reference and payment confirmation details.
  • Uploaded files: Documents, images and other files you submit for a service.
  • Communication information: Messages and inquiries you send through our contact channels.
  • Technical information: Information that may be collected by our hosting provider or website technologies, such as IP address, browser type and access logs.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

  • Register for an account.
  • Log in to our website.
  • Submit a service request.
  • Make or confirm a payment.
  • Upload payment proof or documents.
  • Download completed service results.
  • Contact our support team.
  • Browse or interact with our website.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use information for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing and managing service requests.
  • Confirming and verifying payments.
  • Communicating with customers about their requests.
  • Delivering completed services and files.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Improving the quality and reliability of our services.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

5. Accounts and Passwords

Customers may create accounts to access services. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for activities carried out through your account.

Please use a strong password and do not share it with other people. If you believe that someone has accessed your account without permission, contact us immediately.

We may use account information to authenticate users, manage access and support password-reset requests.

6. Service Requests and Customer Support

When you request a service, we may process the information and instructions you submit so that we can understand, manage and complete your request.

We may contact you using the email address or phone number associated with your account to provide updates, request clarification or communicate about your service.

7. Payments and Financial Information

Pacifique Digital Services may accept payments through supported mobile money services, including MTN MoMo and Airtel Money.

For payment verification, we may process information such as:

  • Payment amount.
  • Payment method.
  • Transaction reference or confirmation details.
  • Payment confirmation screenshots submitted by customers.
  • Payment status and related service information.

We do not ask customers to provide their mobile money PIN or password through our website. Never share your mobile money PIN with anyone.

Mobile money transactions are also subject to the privacy policies and terms of the relevant payment providers.

8. Uploaded Documents and Files

Customers may upload documents, images, payment confirmations or other files required to provide a requested service.

We use these files only for purposes related to the requested service, account administration, payment verification or other legitimate operational needs.

Customers should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive personal information. Please ensure that you have the right to share any document you submit.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customers' personal information. We may share limited information when reasonably necessary for the following purposes:

  • With authorized personnel who need the information to provide services.
  • With hosting, email or technical service providers where necessary to operate the platform.
  • With payment providers when needed to verify or process transactions.
  • When disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful authority.
  • When necessary to protect our users, platform or legal rights.

Where third-party service providers process personal information on our behalf, we will take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate protection.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies to support functions such as login sessions, security and website operation.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and provide consent choices where required by law.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect some website functions.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no website, electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

Customers should protect their account credentials and use secure devices when accessing the platform.

12. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage accounts, resolve disputes, maintain appropriate records and meet legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable law and our operational requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

14. Children's Privacy

Our services are not knowingly directed at children who are not legally able to use the services independently. Where applicable, a parent or legal guardian should provide the necessary authorization.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in circumstances that require authorization and that authorization was not obtained, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

15. Third-Party Services and Websites

Our website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, email delivery, payment services or other technical functions. Links to external websites may also be provided.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies before providing information to external services.

16. International Data Processing

Some service providers that support our website may process or store information outside Rwanda. Where this occurs, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that personal information receives protection in accordance with applicable data protection law.

17. Advertising and Google AdSense

If we display advertisements through Google AdSense or another advertising provider, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Google and its partners may use information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Users can review Google's advertising and privacy information through Google's official privacy resources.

We will update this section and implement any required consent mechanism before enabling advertising features that use non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal obligations or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.
